Currency remembers the selection

With “Remember expense currency” enabled, explicitly selecting a currency immediately remembers it for new expenses, even when the form is closed without saving. Browser storage is scoped to the workspace, with ordered server writes and local fallback during offline failures. Late defaults cannot replace a local choice.

Turning the setting off restores the workspace default. Existing saved expenses retain their own currency when opened for editing.

Dashboard: read-only findings

Current structure

The dashboard leads with a net-worth hero linking to the dedicated wealth screen. The next card emphasizes daily allowance, then monthly budget remaining and spending pace. Recent activity and portfolio, real-asset and category panels follow.

MetricCurrent calculation
Net worthInvestments + real-world assets − active liabilities. This is wealth, not income minus spending.
Monthly budget remainingWorkspace budget, falling back to expected recurring income, minus budget-counted spending. Pending expenses count; net-zero bookkeeping and budget-excluded expenses do not. Split expenses count the user's share.
Daily allowanceNonnegative whole-euro remaining budget divided by days remaining, including today. Overspending is shown separately.
IncomeThe monthly dashboard merges recorded entries and virtual recurring-income forecasts, suppressing salary forecasts when recorded paychecks exist. Late-month salary logic may associate income with a following budget month.
Annual saved / overspentNot currently calculated. It needs year-scoped actual income minus net spending, with a matching total-spent toggle.

Confirmed direction, not implemented

Make monthly budget remaining the primary headline. Below it, show annual income minus spending: positive means saved, negative means overspent. Tapping switches to a clearly labeled total-spent figure for the same period. Reduce wealth to a compact link to its dedicated screen.

Constraints for the next design

  • Annual saved/overspent must use actual income, not recurring forecasts, starting balances or asset valuation gains.
  • Define whether the annual period means year-to-date or a completed calendar year; show that period explicitly.
  • Monthly budget exclusions remain distinct from actual annual spending. Net-zero bookkeeping contributes zero; individual split repayments preserve the user's share.
  • Keep project “Include” overrides out of overall spending calculations.
  • Use consistent currency conversion and timezone boundaries, and avoid reusing the budget's late-payday shifting for actual annual cash-flow reporting.
  • The current server chooses the first member workspace; the new annual query should use the active-workspace helper used by APIs.
  • No dashboard components, layout or metric queries were changed for this review.
