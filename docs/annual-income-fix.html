Annual income correction

The annual query and calculator both excluded every recurring income row. The existing income screen stores a recurring salary once and generates virtual monthly paydays, so that salary disappeared from the annual dashboard.

Locally reproduced and corrected. Production financial records were not retrieved: automatic approval review denied that read. The exact cause and corrected total for the user's account remain unverified.

Deterministic example — sample data

A salary of $4,300, saved as €3,870, starting 15 January 2026, recurring monthly on the 15th, plus €800 of other income. As of 26 September:

€8009 elapsed paydays × €3,870 + €800 = €35,630

Calculation rules

  • Use recorded income and elapsed configured paydays from 1 January through today.
  • Never infer salary before its entered start date or multiply current salary by twelve.
  • Keep each saved EUR amount; no invented historical exchange rates.
  • A separately recorded salary replaces the recurring estimate for its bank account and calendar month, matching the existing monthly-income replacement rule.
  • Paydays count in their receipt month, even when late-month salary funds the next month's budget.
  • Weekly, monthly, quarterly and yearly recurrence is respected; short months cap scheduled days.
  • Future income, unpaid spending and known broker transfers remain excluded. Bookkeeping-only expenses remain net zero.

Limits

Configured recurring paydays are not independently confirmed receipts. The UI explanation now states this explicitly. The data model has no generated-occurrence relationship or salary history/end-date field; actual monthly salary replacement therefore uses the existing account/month rule. Financial records are never rewritten by this fix.
