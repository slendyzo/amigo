Monthly budget — six ways to give it weight

Every frame shows the net-worth hero above the card, because the problem isn't the card's absolute size — it's the size relative to the thing directly above it. Real tokens, real motion, both themes.

What's wrong today: a 13px label, a 12px stat and a 2px bar, stacked under a 34px gradient hero. The eye lands on net worth, slides past the budget, and stops at Upcoming. The budget is the number you act on daily — net worth is the one you check monthly. The hierarchy is inverted.
My pick: C, with D's pace tick folded in. The arc buys real vertical presence without a second gradient competing with the hero, and "left to spend" is the number you actually act on — "spent of budget" is bookkeeping. D's today-marker is the one genuinely new piece of information here: it turns a progress bar into a verdict. F is the boldest reframe and the best fit for the last week of the month, but it hides the total, which will feel disorienting on the 2nd.
