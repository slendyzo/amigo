How it decides: your own category wins first — Viagens & Férias → ✈️, Fast Food → 🍔 — matched against every default category in all three languages plus the legacy names still sitting in old workspaces. If the category is empty or custom, it falls back to keywords in the expense name, so an uncategorised Repsol E1339 still lands on ⛽ and Stay Atenas on 🏨. Other and Uncategorized deliberately resolve to nothing — a ❓ badge is worse than the letter.
