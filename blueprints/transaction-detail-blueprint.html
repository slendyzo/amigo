Design Notes

1.

Centered popup, not bottom sheet

Modal floats in the center on both mobile and desktop. Feels lighter — doesn't anchor to any edge.

2.

75% max-height on mobile

Leaves visible backdrop around all edges. Doesn't take over the screen.

3.

Scale + translate animation

Pops in from scale(0.92) + slight Y offset. Cubic-bezier easing for a springy, native feel.

4.

Softer gradient hero

Replaced the saturated purple-to-indigo with a light #ede9fe → #e0e7ff → #f8fafc wash. Type-tinted but not heavy.

5.

Desktop: 2-column grid

Detail rows pair up in a grid to reduce vertical height. Name left, amount right in the hero.

6.

Tighter spacing throughout

Smaller icons (w-7), less padding, compact rows. Amount at text-3xl instead of 5xl.
